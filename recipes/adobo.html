Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken adobo is a popular Filipino dish made by simmering chicken in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns. It has a savory, slightly sour flavor that makes it delicious when served with rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the chicken, garlic, and other ingredients.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and lightly saute the garlic.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until the outside changes color.
  4. Pour in the soy sauce, vinegar, and water.
  5. Add the bay leaves and black peppercorns.
  6. Simmer until the chicken is tender and the sauce is flavorful.
  7. Serve hot with rice.
